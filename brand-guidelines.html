Proposed digital guidelines — based on the approved design direction; not a replacement for the official brand assets.

01 / Brand positioning

Authentic Thai wellness, refined and contemporary. Use a calm, warm, editorial voice. Prioritize clear booking and package purchase pathways. Avoid exaggerated medical outcomes or undocumented royal lineage. Use Baan d’Or consistently; keep the original official logo artwork when supplied rather than treating the prototype text wordmark as a final logo.

04 / CTA hierarchy

Réserver maintenant →Découvrir nos soins
Réserver ce soin →En savoir plus

One dominant CTA per section. Minimum 44px tap target. Keep focus states visible, use pill buttons, and never rely on color alone. The package bar uses its own contrasting ivory CTA. Use actual links for bookings and native buttons for dialogs.

05 / Photography and motion

Use original Baan d’Or photography only. Mix lifestyle, treatment action and close-up details. Use responsive crops, no embedded text, and appropriate alt text. Hero radius 28px desktop / 20px mobile; cards 22px / 16px; gallery 16px / 12px. Use a restrained single slow zoom, no side chevrons or visible slide numbers, five dots and mobile swipe. Provide pause and reduced-motion support. A real video is a future optional asset, not included.

06 / Layout and mobile

Near-full-width hero with 10–40px outer gutter. Treatment cards: 3 desktop, 2 tablet and standard mobile, 1 below 351 px. Keep main navigation: Treatments, Packages, Our Team, Corporate, Products, About Us, Contact Us, followed by Book Now and language selector. Desktop menu visible from 900 px; mobile menu below.

07 / Content and SEO

Use the master treatment workbook for all nine names, marketing descriptions and SEO subtitles. Separate French and English pages, one H1, unique titles and descriptions, canonical and reciprocal hreflang, clear internal links. Use the supplied 1200 × 630 social image, with an absolute public HTTPS URL. Do not publish until the treatment descriptions and offers are validated.

V16 / Current implementation standards

Use official logo assets/logo.webp. Navigation: Treatments, Packages, Our Team, Corporate, Products, About Us, Contact Us. Gift cards remain in the footer. Booking CTAs go directly to Fresha in a new tab; treatment discovery may use a quick-view, corporate enquiries use an overlay. No intermediate booking dialog. All media use controlled aspect-ratio wrappers: treatment and combined package cards 4:3, therapist portraits 2:3, ritual steps 4:3, social gallery 4:5. On mobile the social gallery scrolls horizontally. Use bronze Instagram CTA and Facebook link. Treatment detail: experience and price table left, booking card right; stack on mobile. Trilogy: 3 × 60 or 3 × 90 minutes from $390, direct package catalogue. Welcome offer: Call us. The V16 website supersedes all historical audits.

V17 final — interaction and imagery

Bronze metallic announcement gradient and fine navigation underline. Cards have warm diffuse shadows, equal heights and aligned CTAs; hover lifts 4 px, CTA scales 2.5% over 250 ms. Respect reduced motion. Use one localized short marketing sentence on treatment cards; keep the full description and SEO/GEO metadata on detail pages. Privilege cards use full-bleed photography with dark readable overlay and champagne CTA. Nuad Yiab uses a bottom-focused crop. Sri La-Ong uses original premium spa imagery; validate exact ritual image before launch.

Direct booking opens Fresha in a new tab, without an intermediate popup. Discover retains quick-view. Treatment cards: three columns on desktop, two on tablet and mobile, one below 351 px. Desktop navigation visible from 900 px. V15 supersedes earlier conflicting specifications.

V18 — Mobile-first interaction standard

Single CTA: whole card clickable. Multiple CTAs: individual buttons only. No CTA: informational card. Header booking: solid chocolate, champagne hover; no flash. Announcement link: discreet underlined text. Treatment quick views: image first, same experience and ideal-for content as detail page, structured prices, booking and full-details CTAs.
