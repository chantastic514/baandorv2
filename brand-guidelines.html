Baan d’Or

Modern Thai luxury. Immersive photography, thoughtful hospitality and a seamless booking experience.

Proposed digital guidelines — based on the approved design direction; not a replacement for the official brand assets.

01 / Brand positioning

Authentic Thai wellness, refined and contemporary. Use a calm, warm, editorial voice. Prioritize clear booking and package purchase pathways. Avoid exaggerated medical outcomes or undocumented royal lineage. Use Baan d’Or consistently; keep the original official logo artwork when supplied rather than treating the prototype text wordmark as a final logo.

04 / CTA hierarchy

Réserver maintenant →Découvrir nos soins
Réserver ce soin →En savoir plus

One dominant CTA per section. Minimum 44px tap target. Keep focus states visible, use pill buttons, and never rely on color alone. The package bar uses its own contrasting ivory CTA. Use actual links for bookings and native buttons for dialogs.

05 / Photography and motion

Use original Baan d’Or photography only. Mix lifestyle, treatment action and close-up details. Use responsive crops, no embedded text, and appropriate alt text. Hero radius 28px desktop / 20px mobile; cards 22px / 16px; gallery 16px / 12px. Use a restrained single slow zoom, no side chevrons or visible slide numbers, five dots and mobile swipe. Provide pause and reduced-motion support. A real video is a future optional asset, not included.

06 / Layout and mobile

Near-full-width hero with 10–40px outer gutter. Use a three-column desktop treatment grid and two-column mobile grid, dropping to one column on very small devices. Keep navigation order: Treatments, Packages & Offers, Gift Cards, Corporate Wellness, Our Story, Contact, then Book Now and language selector. On mobile expose a menu button and prominent booking CTA.

07 / Content and SEO

Use the master treatment workbook for all nine names, marketing descriptions and SEO subtitles. Separate French and English pages, one H1, unique titles and descriptions, canonical and reciprocal hreflang, clear internal links. Use the supplied 1200 × 630 social image, with an absolute public HTTPS URL. Do not publish until the treatment descriptions and offers are validated.

V13 implementation standards

Official supplied logo artwork (assets/logo.webp). Navigation: Treatments, Packages, Our Team, Corporate, About Us, Products, Contact Us. Keep gift cards accessible in the footer, not main navigation.

One card per row on mobile; two on tablet for treatment collections, three on desktop. Use full-width contrasting buttons. Quick-view and booking handoff dialogs are keyboard accessible and fullscreen on mobile. Fresha opens in a separate tab; do not embed without provider authorization. Retain dedicated treatment pages.

Announcement bar: Trilogy — 3 wellness sessions. Instagram CTA: bronze gradient with Instagram icon. Corporate process: four icon-led cards, no press section.

V15 design update

Direct booking opens Fresha in a new tab, without an intermediate popup. Discover retains quick-view. Treatment cards: three columns on desktop, two on tablet and mobile, one below 351 px. Desktop navigation visible from 900 px. V15 supersedes earlier conflicting specifications.
